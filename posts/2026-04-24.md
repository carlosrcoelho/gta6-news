# GTA 6 Daily Report — April 24, 2026

## Official News

**Take-Two Earnings Call Confirmed for May 21**
Take-Two Interactive officially announced its Q4/FY2026 earnings report will be released on **May 21, 2026**, with a conference call beginning at 1:30 PM PT / 4:30 PM ET. Fans are closely watching this date: in May 2025, Take-Two used its earnings period to announce GTA 6's delay and drop Trailer 2. The pattern has many expecting a major GTA 6 announcement around the same window. — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-fans-are-circling-this-date-for-more-news-but-thats-probably-just-wishful-thinking/1100-6539606/)

**Release Date Remains November 19, 2026**
No changes to the official launch date. GTA 6 is confirmed for **November 19, 2026** on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This marks the first fiscal year in which the game is scheduled without a delay announcement. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-official-release-date-update-april-2026/)

**Marketing Push Confirmed for Summer**
Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick reiterated that the GTA 6 marketing campaign will kick off in summer 2026. Pre-orders have not officially opened, though industry sources suggest they may go live around the May 21 earnings call. — [Backyard Drunkard](https://backyarddrunkard.com/games/gta-6-release-date-confirmed-november-2026-updates/)
