## Official News

**Release Date Locked: November 19, 2026**
Take-Two Interactive continues to hold firm on the confirmed launch date of **November 19, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC release has not been officially announced; community speculation points to early 2027. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-official-release-date-update-april-2026/)

**Marketing Campaign Confirmed for Summer 2026**
Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has stated that the GTA 6 marketing push will begin in summer 2026. This is widely interpreted as the likely window for a third official trailer. — [GamesHub](https://www.gameshub.com/news/article/gta-6-official-release-date-update-may-2026-2867205/)

**Take-Two Earnings Call: May 21, 2026**
Take-Two Interactive will release its Q4/FY2026 results on May 21, 2026 at 1:30 PM PT / 4:30 PM ET. Industry analysts expect GTA 6 pricing, pre-order, and marketing details to be addressed during this call. — [Sunday Guardian Live](https://sundayguardianlive.com/tech-news/gta-6-official-release-date-update-sparks-trailer-3-price-reveal-soon-take-two-earnings-call-looms-ahead-of-launch-186448/)

**Rockstar Confirms Data Breach — No Game Impact**
Rockstar officially acknowledged a limited third-party data breach via vendor **Anodot**, stating: *"This incident has no impact on our organization or our players."* GTA 6 source code, gameplay footage, and player passwords were **not** compromised. — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/ahead-of-gta-6-rockstar-hacked-by-ransomware-group-again/1100-6539360/) | [WCCFTech](https://wccftech.com/gta-6-rockstar-confirms-it-was-hacked-shinyhunters-hackers-to-release-stolen-data/)

**AI Has "Zero Part" in GTA 6**
Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick publicly stated that generative AI plays no role in GTA 6's development. — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-company-boss-says-ai-can-be-used-for-evil-but-woe-is-me-risk-is-overblown/1100-6539487/)

## Leaks & Rumors

> Items in this section are **unverified**. Treat all leaks/rumors with appropriate skepticism.

**ShinyHunters Hack — 78.6 Million Records Leaked (April 13–14)**
Hacker group **ShinyHunters** breached Rockstar's Snowflake cloud environment through the third-party vendor Anodot and dumped approximately **7.5 GB** of data after Rockstar declined to pay a reported $200,000 ransom by April 14. The leaked data reportedly includes **8 years of internal KPIs, engagement metrics, and regional revenue data** — but no GTA 6 source code or gameplay footage. — [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/rockstar-games-reportedly-hacked-massive-data-leak-ransom-gta-6-shinyhunters-2000686858) | [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/rockstar-gta6-hack-data-leak/)

**Gameplay Clip Surfaces Online (April 10)**
A short, unverified gameplay video purportedly from an early-to-mid development build began circulating on Reddit, X, and YouTube around April 10. The footage reportedly shows Vice City streets, environmental detail, and character animation. Authenticity has **not** been confirmed by Rockstar. — [The Experiment](https://md-eksperiment.org/en/post/20260413-gta-6-leaked-gameplay-video-april-2026-what-the-new-clip-actually-shows) | [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-gameplay-leak-open-world-size/)

**Updated Community Map Leak (April 14)**
An updated version of the fan-constructed GTA VI map circulated online, significantly more detailed than the 2022 original. It reportedly depicts motorways, towns, countryside, mountains, and confirms Vice City as a major urban hub. Community analysis suggests the total playable area may be **roughly twice the size of GTA V**, with Vice City itself comprising less than 20% of the map. — [Gaming Bible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-updated-map-leak-324914-20260414) | [Attack of the Fanboy](https://attackofthefanboy.com/gaming/gta-6s-updated-community-map-just-hit-reddit-and-fans-are-already-saying-goodbye-to-their-social-lives-before-it-even-launches/)

**Wanted System Details (Resurfaced Leak)**
A circulating leak claims GTA 6's police Wanted system will be a major overhaul from GTA 5, featuring smarter police AI, dynamic "search zones," and more nuanced escalation mechanics. Source and original leak date unconfirmed. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-leaked-wanted-system/)

**Trailer 3 Speculation Dates**
The Twitter/X account **@GTAVI_Countdown** posted a list of speculative Trailer 3 reveal windows: **April 28, May 5, May 12, May 14, May 26, and June 9**. No official confirmation exists. The May 21 earnings call is the strongest candidate per multiple gaming journalists. — [X / GTAVI_Countdown](https://x.com/GTAVI_Countdown/status/2040058792692391947) | [Gaming Bible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-trailer-3-dropping-350350-20260402)

**Third Delay Fears Fading — Prediction Markets at 28%**
As of April 1, prediction markets showed only **28% of bettors** expecting a third delay, down sharply from 55% in January. Take-Two's FY2027 forecast is built entirely around a November launch. Most analysts consider another delay unlikely. — [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2026/04/01/gta-6-bets-delay-fan-anxiety-grand-theft-auto-vi/) | [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-delay-again-2026/)

## Community Highlights

**Map Scale Fan Analysis Goes Viral**
Reddit and gaming outlets picked up a fan analysis showing that a single in-game screenshot of a beach/bridge location contains approximately **14,287 individually rendered trees**. Separately, leaks suggest the game will feature **700+ shops**, many with enterable interiors, as well as shopping malls and skyscrapers with functional elevators. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-tree-map-graphics-details-fan-reaction/) | [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/gta-6-leaks-reveal-major-changes-ahead-of-release-as-fan-hype-intensifies-1.500510196)

**Jason & Lucia Relationship Bar Discussion**
Reddit communities (r/GTA6, GTAForums) are actively debating a reported "relationship bar" mechanic between protagonists Jason and Lucia, with speculation that player choices could influence story outcomes. Also discussed: the apparent removal of a "prone" stealth position seen in earlier leaks. — [GTAForums](https://gtaforums.com/forum/396-gta-vi/)

**Rockstar GTA Online Roadmap Void Sparks Trailer Speculation**
Community members on r/GTA6 noted that Rockstar's GTA Online weekly update schedule went unusually quiet after April 1, fueling ongoing speculation that Rockstar is preparing bandwidth for a major GTA 6 announcement. — [GamesHub](https://www.gameshub.com/news/article/news-gta-6-announcement-rumors-april-2026-rockstar-games-2861082/)
