# GTA VI Daily Report — April 26, 2026

## Official News

**No Official Announcements Today**
Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive made no new statements on April 26. Release date remains **November 19, 2026** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

**Delay Probability at All-Time Low**
Prediction market data shows fan confidence in the November release at its highest point yet, with only **28% of bettors** expecting another delay — down from 55% in January 2026. Take-Two's FY2027 revenue forecast is built entirely around a November launch window, giving analysts strong confidence. — [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-delay-again-2026/) | [PlayStation LifeStyle](https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/2026/04/01/gta-6-bets-delay-fan-anxiety-grand-theft-auto-vi/)

## Leaks & Rumors

> Items in this section are **unverified**. Treat with appropriate skepticism.

**GTA VI Time Period Leak — Set Partly in the Past?**
Gaming Bible reported on a leak (originally surfaced April 13) suggesting GTA VI may feature story segments set in a different time period than the present day, pointing to "an unexpected journey into the past." Details remain vague and unverified. Rockstar has not commented. — [Gaming Bible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-time-period-leak-unexpected-journey-411306-20260413)

**"Correct" Leaks Compiled — Rockstar Employee Hint?**
Gaming Bible also highlighted a compilation of GTA VI leaks that a supposed Rockstar-adjacent source described as pointing in the right direction, without specifying which. The post has circulated widely but cannot be independently verified. — [Gaming Bible](https://www.gamingbible.com/games/gta/gta-6-leaks-compiled-rockstar-employee-942378-20260119)

**Delay Leak Resurfaces — But Dismissed by Most**
A Screen Rant report revisited a circulating rumor that GTA VI could still face a third delay, citing unnamed sources. However, the piece notes the rumor conflicts with multiple official signals from Take-Two and is considered low-credibility by most analysts. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-delay-leaks-april-2026/)

**Most Likely True Leaks — Community Roundup**
Screen Rant published a ranked list of GTA VI leaks considered most credible by the community, including: a more dynamic wanted system, extensive interior environments, dual protagonist story interplay, and a map roughly twice the size of GTA 5. All remain unconfirmed. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-leaks-most-likely-true-accurate-real/)

## Community Highlights

**All Eyes on April 28**
The GTA VI community is in a heightened state of anticipation heading into the weekend, with April 28 widely circulated as a potential announcement date. The date has no official backing — it stems from Rockstar's unusual GTA Online schedule gap and fan pattern-matching. GameSpot cautions it is likely "wishful thinking." — [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-fans-are-circling-this-date-for-more-news-but-thats-probably-just-wishful-thinking/1100-6539606/)

**GTA VI Delay Fears vs. Excitement — A Community Snapshot**
PCQuest published a breakdown of the current community mood: excitement is dominant, delay anxiety is fading, and Trailer 3 speculation is the central topic driving engagement across Reddit, YouTube, and X. — [PCQuest](https://www.pcquest.com/gaming/gta-6-release-date-keeps-fans-on-the-edgedelay-fears-vs-excitement-11750041)

**PC Release Timeline Discussion**
r/GTA6 threads are actively debating when the PC version will arrive. Prevailing theory: 12–18 months after the console launch, placing it between late 2027 and mid-2028. Rockstar has not announced a PC version. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/gta-6-release-date-setting-map-characters-gameplay-trailers)
