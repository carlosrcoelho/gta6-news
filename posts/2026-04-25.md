# GTA 6 Daily Report — April 25, 2026

**No Official Announcements Today**
Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive made no new official statements on April 25. The confirmed release date remains **November 19, 2026** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Take-Two's previously confirmed summer marketing window remains the key milestone to watch. No trailer or pre-order date has been officially set. **Trailer 3 Around Earnings Call — Growing Consensus**
Multiple gaming outlets now point to the May 21 earnings call window as the most likely moment for Trailer 3. Precedent from 2025 supports this: Rockstar dropped Trailer 2 just before Take-Two's Q4 earnings that year. No official confirmation exists. — [Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/gta-6-trailer-3-may-2026-earnings-call/)

**GTA 6 Price Speculation: $80–$100?**
Community discussion about GTA 6's launch price is intensifying. With Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick previously hinting at "recalibrating" pricing for major releases, many expect GTA 6 to launch at $80 or even $100 for the base game. No price has been officially announced. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/gta-6-release-date-setting-map-characters-gameplay-trailers)

**Compiled Confirmed Features List**
Gaming outlets have published updated roundups of confirmed GTA 6 features, including: dual protagonists Jason and Lucia, the fictional state of Leonida (based on Florida), Vice City as the main hub, and the largest open world in series history. — [GameLuster](https://gameluster.com/what-makes-gta-6-special-every-confirmed-new-featu/)
